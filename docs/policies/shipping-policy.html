Processing time

Orders are picked, checked and dispatched within 2 business days. Orders placed on a Sunday or a public holiday are processed on the next working day.

Delivery time

Once dispatched, Standard delivery within India usually takes 3–7 business days and Express 1–3 business days. International orders usually take 10–21 business days. These are estimates from our courier partners rather than guarantees — weather, strikes and customs clearance can add time.

Tracking

You'll get an email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves us. If that link hasn't updated within 48 hours of dispatch, write to {{ email }} and we'll chase it with the courier.

Customs, duties and taxes

Prices on our site include Indian GST. For international orders, any import duties, taxes or customs handling charges are set by the destination country and are payable by the recipient. We cannot calculate these in advance, and they are not refundable by us.

Incomplete or incorrect addresses

Please check your address and phone number before you pay. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incomplete or nobody was available to receive it, we'll contact you to arrange redelivery; the second shipping charge is payable by you.

Lost or damaged in transit

If your parcel arrives damaged, or doesn't arrive at all, tell us within 7 days of the expected delivery date at {{ email }}. We'll open a claim with the courier and replace or refund the order.
