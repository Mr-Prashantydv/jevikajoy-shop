Return window

You have 7 days from the day your order is delivered to request a return or an exchange.

What we can accept back

To be eligible, the item must be:

  • unworn and unwashed
  • free of stains, odours and pet hair
  • with all original tags still attached
  • in its original packaging

We can't accept items that have been worn, washed or altered, or items returned after the 7-day window has closed.

Exchanges

Ordered the wrong size? Your first exchange on any order is free — we'll send the new size and arrange collection of the original at no cost to you, subject to that size being in stock.

How to start a return

Email {{ email }} with your order number, and a photo if the item is damaged or incorrect. We'll confirm and send you return instructions. Please don't post anything back before we've confirmed — an unannounced parcel can't be matched to your order, which delays your refund.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send a photo. We'll replace the item or refund it in full including shipping, and we won't ask you to pay to send it back.

Refunds

Once your return reaches us and passes inspection, we'll refund the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take a few days more to show it. Original shipping charges are refunded only where the return is our error.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order at no cost any time before it is dispatched — email {{ email }} as soon as you can. Once a parcel is with the courier it has to be handled as a return.
